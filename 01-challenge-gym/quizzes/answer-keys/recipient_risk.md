# Quiz answer key — recipient risk

Use only after attempting the quiz questions.

1. Invalid/typo addresses and mixed sets are different risks; “any external” alone cannot express “all external is OK for this partner mail” vs “mixed is suspicious.”
2. A message meant for colleagues may also leave the org — classic autocomplete / CC mistake that DLP holds can interrupt.
3. Same habit, different attacker: missend is accidental; BEC is attacker-driven fraud (spoof/hijack). Controls and playbooks differ even when classification overlaps.
4. Domains are case-insensitive; naive compare false-negatives “same” addresses and mis-labels internal as external.
5. Keeps the kata testable and interview-sized; accepts that display-names / quoted locals are out of scope.
6. **Mixed** — after normalization, one internal (`alex@mailguard.example`) and one external.
